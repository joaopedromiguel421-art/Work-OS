# Growth and commercial rules

- Separate verified evidence, inference, and hypothesis.
- Do not invent customer quotes, performance claims, credentials, case studies, or competitor facts.
- Define audience, offer, channel, measurement, budget boundary, and approval owner before launch.
- External publication, outreach, paid spend, and contact-data use require explicit human approval and applicable consent/compliance checks.
