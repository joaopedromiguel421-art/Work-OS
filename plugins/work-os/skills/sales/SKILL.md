# Sales

## Method

1. Define the commercial decision, account/opportunity stage, stakeholders, desired next step, evidence, constraints, and system of record.
2. For research, use public/authorized sources and separate verified facts from inferred priorities. Do not infer sensitive personal traits.
3. For qualification/discovery, map problem, impact, current approach, urgency, decision process, stakeholders, technical/commercial constraints, alternatives, and mutual next step.
4. For outreach or follow-up, ground every personalized statement in a source, lead with relevance, make one clear ask, and keep claims within approved proof.
5. For deal strategy, map stakeholder roles, value, risks, objections, dependencies, competition/status quo, mutual action plan, and exit criteria.
6. For proposals/business cases, make assumptions, costs, benefits, timing, risk, and evidence explicit. Do not disguise estimates as commitments.
7. For pipeline/forecast, use defined stage and evidence criteria; report confidence, aging, data quality, and scenario ranges.

## Integration boundary

CRM, prospecting, email, calendar, enrichment, or calling tools are optional MCPs/plugins. Use them only after project opt-in, least-privilege review, correct-recipient verification, and explicit approval for writes or communication.

## Guardrails

Never auto-send, bulk-contact, purchase data, change a CRM, promise price/terms, or publish a proposal without approval. Respect consent, suppression lists, applicable law, and platform terms. Do not invent customer facts or quotes.

## Companions

Use `market-intelligence` for landscape evidence, `positioning-and-offer` for the strategic offer, and `copy-and-content` for reusable collateral. Sales owns account/opportunity application.

## Output

Evidence brief, stakeholder/deal map, recommended action, draft artifact, assumptions/risks, approval points, and proposed system-of-record update.
