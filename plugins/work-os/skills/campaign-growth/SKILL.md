# Campaign growth

## Method

1. Define objective, audience, journey stage, offer, geography, timing, constraints, baseline, budget ceiling, and owner.
2. Verify positioning and claims. Invoke `positioning-and-offer` before channel work if the offer is unresolved.
3. Select channels from audience behavior, fit, economics, creative requirements, measurement reliability, and operating capacity—not trend alone.
4. Build a campaign architecture: message pillars, channel roles, creative concepts, landing destination, sequencing, exclusions, and lifecycle follow-up.
5. Use `copy-and-content` for assets and `landing-page` for the conversion destination when needed.
6. Define instrumentation, attribution caveats, primary outcome, leading indicators, guardrails, reporting cadence, and decision thresholds through `analytics-measurement`.
7. Create launch QA for links, parameters, audiences, consent, frequency, budgets, creative variants, destinations, and rollback/pause.
8. Obtain explicit approval before spend, account changes, upload of audiences/contact data, publication, or launch.
9. Review by cohort/channel/creative with uncertainty and incrementality limits; document learnings and next action.

## Guardrails

No fabricated proof, hidden sponsorship, discriminatory targeting, unauthorized personal data, deceptive scarcity, or evasion of platform rules. Do not expose credentials in campaign artifacts.

## Output

Campaign brief, audience/channel rationale, message/creative matrix, destination plan, budget and approval boundaries, measurement, QA, launch/pause criteria, and learning cadence.
