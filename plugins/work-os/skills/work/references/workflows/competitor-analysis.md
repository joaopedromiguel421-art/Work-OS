# Competitor analysis workflow

## Entry

- Required: strategic decision, category/segment, geography, comparison date, named competitors or discovery rule, and comparison dimensions.

## Main-conversation sequence

1. Use `market-intelligence` to define direct, adjacent, substitute, and status-quo alternatives plus a normalized evidence frame.
2. Use `evidence-research` rules for source quality, dates, contradictions, and fact/inference separation.
3. Partition independent competitor/source clusters to `research-scout` only when scope warrants; give each the same normalized table.
4. Verify official product, pricing, docs, filings/announcements, and credible customer evidence. Normalize editions, currencies, billing periods, regions, and versions.
5. Synthesize patterns, strengths, constraints, whitespace, risks, and implications. Do not turn absence of public evidence into a weakness claim.
6. Use `positioning-and-offer` only after evidence collection if the requested decision concerns positioning.
7. Store a dated brief and freshness limit; update the research index when reusable.

## Parallelism

Maximum two scouts concurrently and three total. Divide by competitor cluster or source type; never duplicate the same company merely to vote. The main conversation owns the comparison and recommendation.

## Complete when

Every material cell is sourced or marked unknown; date/version normalization is visible; allegations and private-data inference are absent; strategic implications connect to the entry decision; refresh triggers are stated.
