# Offer creation workflow

## Entry

- Required: target market/customer evidence, product/service capabilities, desired outcome, delivery constraints, proof, cost/economic assumptions, and decision owner.

## Main-conversation sequence

1. Read product, user, brand, market, metrics, sales, and delivery-capacity context.
2. Use `product-discovery` or `market-intelligence` to resolve a material user/alternative gap; `research-scout` may collect bounded external evidence.
3. Use `positioning-and-offer` to select ICP/exclusions, category, differentiated value, proof, package, price hypothesis, risk boundaries, qualification, and next action.
4. Stress-test the offer against deliverability, capacity, unit economics, compliance, support burden, and unsupported claims.
5. Use `copy-and-content` only after the strategic offer is accepted to create a message hierarchy or sales collateral.
6. Define validation: interviews, proposal tests, pricing research, landing experiment, or sales evidence, with guardrails.
7. Record assumptions and owner approvals. Do not publish pricing, guarantees, discounts, or purchase commitments automatically.

## Parallelism

Customer/market evidence and internal cost/capacity analysis may proceed independently in read-only branches, maximum two; final packaging is dependent and remains in the main conversation.

## Quality gates

Evidence and claim checks, economic assumption review, delivery-capacity review, and the standard repository profile must pass for any implemented artifact. Price, guarantee, or commercial commitment requires the named human owner.

## Complete when

Target/exclusions, value/proof, scope, package, price assumptions, risk/claim limits, qualification, economics/capacity, and validation plan are explicit; human-owned commercial commitments remain approved or pending.
