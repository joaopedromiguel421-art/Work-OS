# Campaign workflow

## Entry

- Required: objective, audience, offer, journey stage, channels under consideration, geography/timing, budget ceiling, brand/compliance constraints, and approval owner.

## Main-conversation sequence

1. Read product, users, brand, market evidence, metrics, and current offer.
2. Use `market-intelligence` for a material channel/audience evidence gap and `positioning-and-offer` until the offer/claims are accepted.
3. Use `campaign-growth` to define channel roles, message/creative system, sequencing, destination, budget boundaries, QA, and pause criteria.
4. Use `copy-and-content` for drafts, `landing-page` for the destination, and `analytics-measurement` for metric/event/attribution contracts.
5. Build a launch checklist covering links/parameters, audiences/consent, frequency, budgets, creative, destination states, ownership, and rollback.
6. Keep every asset/file write in the main conversation. Do not connect accounts, upload audiences, publish, or spend.
7. Run deterministic asset/link/schema/build checks and obtain explicit approval for every external launch action.

## Parallelism

Independent channel evidence branches may use up to two `research-scout` agents. Creative/copy variants are dependent on one claim system and stay in main. Post-launch analysis is a later authorized step.

## Quality gates

Asset, link, parameter, consent, destination build, and tracking-schema checks must pass. Human approval is the blocking gate for spend, account changes, audience upload, publication, or launch.

## Complete when

Campaign brief and asset matrix are complete; claims/consent are approved; destination and tracking pass QA; budget/pause/owner are explicit; all external actions are either approved and performed through an authorized integration or clearly handed off as pending.
